    Reference

    I used AI on this project. Here are the prompts I gave it and what I used them for.

    My role

    I directed this project like a product owner. I decided what the site needed, planned the pages and features from the assignment requirements, and told the AI what to build. The AI did the building and checking I asked for. I reviewed the results, had it check my work against the Canvas rubric and project description, and decided what to fix. The prompts below are the instructions I gave it.

    AI tool

    Claude (Anthropic), used through Claude Code.

    Prompts

    Prompt 1: Front-end build

    I'm doing the front-end phase of my CIS 2336 web project, the UH Sugar Land Student Center website. Using the starter files (index.html, README.md, app.js, .gitignore) and the assignment description, build the front-end: a homepage with a dynamic navigation menu and a "Developer Contact" section with my name and UH email, an FAQ page with 5 collapsible items, a room booking form for 5 rooms with form validation, a reference page that displays my AI prompts, and images or remotely hosted video. Follow the project structure in the README.

    Used for: first draft of the HTML pages, stylesheet, and script.js. I reviewed and tested the result.

    Prompt 2: Check my work against the assignment

1.
